About

Where data meets decisions.

Sarayu is a third-year BSc Economics with Data Science student at the University of Southampton, on track for a first-class degree. Her degree gives her the technical grounding; her experience as a university rowing coxswain has built the fast thinking, communication, and calm leadership needed under pressure.

She is especially drawn to roles where data meets strategy - across banking, fintech, and consultancy - translating analysis into decisions, understanding how products and teams fit together, and communicating complex findings clearly to different audiences.

She's also built hands-on AI and LLM projects, including a retrieval-augmented generation system and a prompt-engineering study, to understand how these tools are actually built rather than just how to use them.
